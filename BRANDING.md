## About me

Paste this into Developer Portal → General Information → Description (max 400 characters). Discord shows it as the bot's About me.

```text
⚡ Neon-charged music for your server. /play anything, synthwave & 80s radio, bassboost · nightcore, saved playlists. Free forever, Premium for more. Dashboard: krishyaa06.github.io/prism-bots-panel
```
